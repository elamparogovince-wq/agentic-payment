Demo, data & accessibility

Prototype only: delivery readings and locations are sample inputs, not verified sensor or GPS evidence. Payments use Stellar Testnet tokens, which have no real-world value. Do not enter personal or sensitive addresses.

External services: map tiles and place searches are provided by OpenStreetMap. When you search, your query is sent to its Nominatim service; opening the map also requests map tiles. The demo stores a temporary shipment ID in this browser session and sends delivery scenario inputs to the AeroPay server. Velo checkout opens separately when selected.

No refund or production payment policy applies to this prototype. No analytics or advertising tracker is included in the demo page. You can use the main controls by keyboard; visible focus indicates the current control.
